## Cover prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: premium GitHub repository cover, landscape 1536 by 1024.
Create a polished editorial 3D illustration for "GaariGar", an actual roadside assistance software project connecting drivers, mechanics and fuel stations. A crafted miniature city sits on an off-white architectural presentation board: a winding cobalt blue road connects a stranded compact car, a mechanic's small garage with a wrench symbol, and a tidy fuel station. A tiny service vehicle travels along the route. Use soft clay-like matte materials, precise geometric modeling, beautifully composed isometric perspective, subtle long shadows, warm cream backdrop, cobalt blue, dark navy, small yellow accents. Strong visual hierarchy, generous negative space in the upper left for the title. The road visually connects the three actors, with a few discreet blue location pins. Not a screenshot or an app interface. Sophisticated, restrained and memorable, like a designed product launch editorial cover, never clip art.
Text exactly: "GaariGar" as a large confident navy sans-serif title at upper left; beneath it "Roadside assistance, connected." in smaller type. Bottom left small line "CUSTOMERS / MECHANICS / FUEL STATIONS".
No other text, no metrics, no badges, no third-party logos, no fake phone interfaces, no watermarks. Keep all typography readable and entirely within generous safe margins. This is a concept illustration, not a claim of operational service.
